# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all mobile applications published by Mike Kennedy ("the Developer").

**The Developer operates no servers and receives no user data.** No application sends
any information to the Developer, and the Developer has no ability to access your data.

## Per-app policies

Some applications connect to third-party services that you choose to sign in to. Where
that is the case, the app has its own policy describing exactly what is sent and to whom.
These per-app policies take precedence over this one:

- [DevFlow](DevFlow.md) — Azure DevOps client

Applications with no per-app policy listed above process all data locally on your device
and transmit nothing.

## Applies to all apps

- No analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or crash-reporting SDKs are used.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising, and no profile is built
  about you.
- Credentials and settings are stored in the operating system's secure credential store
  (iOS/macOS Keychain, Android Keystore, Windows credential storage), and are removed
  when you sign out or delete the app.
- The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from
  them.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before release if any app's data handling changes.
